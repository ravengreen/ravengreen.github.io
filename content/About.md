I am driven by an interest in human behaviour, culture, and society, committed to understanding how social coexistence can be supported.

My early work focused on information design, specialising in visual communication. I found it deeply rewarding to translate specialist jargon and complex content into clear, accessible visual forms that enable people to understand, align, and make informed decisions.

During  my studies I came across digital and interactive technologies as a medium for my work, which opened up a whole new world of design possibilities. To merge my technological affinity with my creativity and passion for the social, I shifted my focus to human-computer interaction, developing stronger conceptual and methodological skills.

Turning from pure communication to the active shaping of living environments, I currently work across a broad range of topics with a special interest in designing IT that supports collaboration, participation and digital wellbeing.
